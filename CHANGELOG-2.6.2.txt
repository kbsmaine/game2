DEADHAUL BUILD 2.6.2

- Rebuilt enemy death tuning for stable, grounded ragdolls.
- Removed direct 3.4 m/s corpse launch velocity and random high angular velocity.
- Bullet impacts now apply only a subtle chest impulse; gravity creates the fall.
- Reduced ragdoll collision proxy sizes to avoid activation overlap.
- Disabled self-collision between body parts while preserving collision with the level.
- Set ragdoll restitution to zero and increased body friction.
- Added aggressive angular damping and sensible linear damping.
- Added a 0.75 second stabilization phase that caps solver spikes after animation-to-physics handoff.
- Added ongoing velocity caps during corpse settling to prevent endless flailing.
- Calmed the non-Havok fallback death behavior as well.
- Preserved Build 2.6.1 split safehouse UI, persistent stash/display, bunker customization, inventory actions and all combat/animation systems.
- Fresh local test port: 8806.
