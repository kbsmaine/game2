DEADHAUL Build 2.6.0 — Safehouse / Stash / Body Pass

Major additions
- Extracted raid loot now goes into a persistent safehouse stash instead of disappearing into an inaccessible post-raid summary.
- Added in-bunker stash/loadout panel. Press TAB in the safehouse or use the stash interaction near the shelves.
- Added prepared raid backpack system: move items from stash into the bag before deploying, then bring those items into the raid.
- Added equipment management from the stash: equip/unequip helmets, armor, rigs, backpacks, primary weapons and sidearms.
- Added item dropping from the raid backpack. Dropped items spawn physically in the world and can be picked back up.
- Added inventory/item thumbnails for weapons, ammo, meds, food, gear, tools and valuables.
- Added safehouse display slots for recovered items and a simple safehouse customization panel with theme and lighting controls.
- Added first-person chest/torso proxy and reduced downward pitch so looking down feels more like a shooter instead of looking through the legs.
- Added tactile UI sounds and footstep sounds.
- Improved enemy death physics with a collision-aware ragdoll-style body flop and searchable corpse trigger following the body.

Persistence
- Demo mode supports stash insert/delete for equip, unequip and prepared bag actions.
- Supabase schema now includes inventory insert/delete policies for authenticated players.
