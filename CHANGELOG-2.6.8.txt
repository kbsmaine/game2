DEADHAUL BUILD 2.6.8 — TACTICAL CONTAINER INVENTORY

- Rebuilt field inventory into Tarkov-style equipment + rig + pockets + backpack containers.
- Chest rigs now have real cell counts: Utility 4, Security 6, Tactical 8, Assault 10.
- Backpacks now have cell counts in addition to weight capacity: 8 / 20 / 30 / 42.
- Added eight detachable magazine item types for supported pistol/SMG/rifle/shotgun weapons.
- Compatible magazines stored in rig/pockets/backpack are consumed on reload; HUD displays spare magazines.
- Tactical loot auto-routes to rig, then pockets, then backpack when picked up.
- Drag/drop and double-click quick transfer between field containers.
- Quartermaster loadout preparation now separately shows chest rig, pockets and backpack contents.
- Prepared tactical items persist in loadout and enter raids in their selected containers.
- Extracted items include contents carried in rig/pockets/backpack.
- New magazine SVG inventory icons.
