DEADHAUL BUILD 2.6.12 — PERSISTENT MAGAZINES + LOOSE AMMUNITION

- Magazines are unique persistent containers with real round contents.
- Standard, extended and drum variants added across detachable-mag weapons.
- Magazine manager UI: load/unload loose rounds and insert compatible magazines.
- Active weapon inventory panel shows/ejects/manages inserted magazine.
- R reload uses a real loaded spare mag and preserves partial magazines.
- Loose ammo stacks persist their remaining round count.
- 12 gauge: 00 buckshot, rifled slug, flechette; mixed Saiga mags supported.
- Pump shotgun loads loose shells individually and retains shell order.
- Multi-cell extended/drum mags consume additional rig/backpack space.
- Dropped magazines retain their contents.
- Extraction/stash persistence includes item_data metadata.
- Added Supabase migration sql/MIGRATION-2.6.12.sql.
