DEADHAUL BUILD 2.6.5 — SOFT RAGDOLL DEATHS

- Replaced the rigid kill-frame collapse from 2.6.4 with a hybrid soft-ragdoll simulation.
- Enemies briefly blend out of run/aim into their weapon-class idle pose before the collapse begins.
- Knees buckle first; the torso then drops with no world-space launch impulse.
- Arms, head, hands, legs and feet use damped angular spring motion for soft secondary movement.
- No live Havok skeletal chain is activated on impact, avoiding solver explosions and flying bodies.
- Corpses settle independently and are never frozen by another enemy death.
- SEARCH BODY continues to follow the fallen torso.
- All Build 2.6.4 stash, bunker, inventory, audio and player-body systems are retained.
